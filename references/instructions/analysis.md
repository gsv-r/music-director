# Guidelines for analysis

Analysis is short prose for the user, written after the three delivered fields.
It explains the finished result so the user can understand the composition. It
is not a fifth musical field, does not generate audio and never exposes private
reasoning. See `references/instructions/orchestration.md` for universal rules
and "Final delivery format (mandatory)" for presentation.

## What it does

Explain, only when pertinent:

- what the piece proposes and the context from the brief;
- what the lyrics say and how the speaker is positioned;
- the emotional movement across the song;
- what the hook concentrates;
- how the result answers the brief, including any divergence.

A short, simple piece needs only two or three of these. Use section roles only
when they help the user understand the result. Do not force a narrative arc
onto abstract or non-narrative writing.

## Rules

- Every concrete claim is traceable to the delivered text or to the brief.
  Re-read the final version before writing.
- Present what is explicit as fact and interpretation as possibility: "may
  suggest", "one reading is". Never present an inference as an observation.
- Do not invent events, characters, relationships, meanings or reasons for a
  choice. Do not attribute the speaker's knowledge to the user.
- Cite at most one short line of the lyric when it anchors a point. Do not
  reproduce the lyric, restate the Style line or repeat the section marks.
- Avoid generic praise. Specific observations only.
- Stay proportional: the analysis should not be longer than the piece it
  explains without a reason.
- If Style, lyrics, form or marks do not fit together, report the discrepancy
  here rather than silently rewriting the delivered fields.

## Language

Write in the language used to communicate with the user, unless they request
another. Keep short quotations in the language of the lyrics and do not
translate them unless asked.

## Pre-delivery check

Before delivering, confirm that the analysis is faithful to the final
composition, that interpretations are marked as possible, that nothing was
invented, that no narrative arc was forced, that no content from the other
fields was duplicated, and that no private reasoning appears. One pass is
enough; report an unresolved discrepancy instead of looping.
