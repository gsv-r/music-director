# Vocal dynamics reference

Local vocabulary for dynamic shape. Rules and tag limits live in
`references/instructions/performance.md`. Consult via `index.md`.

### Crescendo and diminuendo

Gradual increase or decrease of loudness. Sounds like a build or a fade.
Use to shape a phrase or a section's movement. Specify whether it comes from
execution or from added layers; the latter is an arrangement decision.

### Dynamic contrast

A clear move between soft and loud. Sounds like intensity changing at a
specific point. Use to mark a turn in the emotion or a section boundary. Avoid
when the intent is stable and even.

### Accent

Extra emphasis on a note or beat. Sounds like a pointed attack. Use to clarify
groove, stress a word or create rhythmic tension. Avoid overuse, which flattens
the hierarchy of accents.
