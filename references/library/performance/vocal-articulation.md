# Vocal articulation reference

Local vocabulary for how notes are connected and shaped. Rules and tag limits
live in `references/instructions/performance.md`. Consult via `index.md`.

### Legato

Smooth connection between notes, with no breaks between syllables. Sounds flow
and sustained. Use for melodic lines and long phrases. Avoid when the groove
needs separation or the words need percussive delivery.

### Staccato

Short, detached notes with space between them. Sounds crisp and articulated.
Use for rhythmic phrasing and playful or tense lines. Avoid for sustained
emotion or legato melodies.

### Twang

Bright, forward, slightly nasal quality with a focused tone. Sounds present and
cutting. Use in rock, country, musical theatre and belted passages. Avoid when
a warm or intimate sound is wanted; it conflicts with breathy or whispered
delivery. Source: Estill voice technique (convention).
