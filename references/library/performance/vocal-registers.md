# Vocal registers reference

Local vocabulary for register and emission. Rules and tag limits live in
`references/instructions/performance.md`. Consult via `index.md`.

### Chest voice

Chest-dominant, fuller and more grounded tone. Sounds warm and present. Use for
power, directness and low or middle ranges. Avoid forcing it upward without
technique.

### Head voice

Lighter, higher coordination with resonance in the head. Sounds clear and
floating, not breathy. Use for lift and ease in upper phrases. Avoid when weight
and power are wanted.

### Falsetto

Light, airy upper emission above the normal modal range. Sounds ethereal or
intimate. Use for contrast and delicate passages. Avoid where a solid tone is
needed.

### Mixed voice

Blend of chest and head coordination across the middle. Sounds even, with
projection and no break. Use to carry a line through the passaggio. Source:
Miller, *The Structure of Singing* (1996); conventional vocal pedagogy.
