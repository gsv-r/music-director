# Phrasing and timing reference

Local vocabulary for expressive timing. Rules and tag limits live in
`references/instructions/performance.md`. Consult via `index.md`.

### Rubato

Flexible tempo inside a phrase, with notes pushed or held back. Sounds
expressive and speech-like. Use in ballads and solo passages. Avoid when a
strict groove is the identity. Source: Miller, *The Structure of Singing* (1996).

### Push and pull

Placing a phrase slightly ahead of or behind the beat. Sounds human and
laid-back or urgent. Use to give a groove a natural feel. Keep it subtle; do not
turn it into a tempo change.

### Breath points

Planned places to breathe inside or between phrases. Sounds like natural
phrasing with room to sing. Use to make long lines singable. Avoid breaks that
cut the meaning of a line.

### Behind and ahead of the beat

Consistent placement of delivery against the pulse. Behind sounds relaxed and
laid-back; ahead sounds urgent. Use to define feel within a genre. Avoid when
it conflicts with the established groove.
