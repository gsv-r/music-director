# Vocal ornaments and effects reference

Local vocabulary for audible detail on a note. Rules and tag limits live in
`references/instructions/performance.md`. Consult via `index.md`.

### Vibrato

Small, regular pitch oscillation on a sustained note. Sounds warm and alive.
Use on longer notes and emotional peaks. Avoid on every note; straight tone can
be more expressive. Source: Sundberg (1987).

### Runs

Fast successive notes on one syllable or across a short phrase. Sounds agile
and ornate. Use in pop, soul and R&B where the genre supports it. Avoid in
dense storytelling or minimalist writing.

### Melisma

Several notes sung on a single syllable. Sounds expressive and extended. Use
at a key word or a held emotional moment. Avoid when it blurs the lyric.

### Growl

Rasp or grit added to the tone. Sounds rough and urgent. Use in rock, blues and
aggressive passages. Avoid in soft or clean contexts; it conflicts with breathy
delivery.

### Cry or sob

A tight, emotional quality with a slight catch in the tone. Sounds vulnerable.
Use at emotional peaks in ballads, soul and R&B. Avoid when the line needs a
clear, steady tone.
